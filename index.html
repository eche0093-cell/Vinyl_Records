Chapter 1

The comeback

Vinyl sales have surged since 2011. ARIA records what Australians buy each year. These three charts track the revival of vinyl collecting. Together they show growth, a milestone and a shift.

Chapter 2

What gets pressed

Discogs catalogues records from around the world. We counted Australian vinyl releases by year and genre. Counts show releases, not sales. These charts compare genres and release years. They reveal how music preferences shifted across decades.

Who makes the music
