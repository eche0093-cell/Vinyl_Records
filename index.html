The Vinyl Renaissance in Australia

An interactive exploration of record releases, genre evolution, industry revenues, and store density across states.

Total Record Releases Over Time

Australian vinyl pressings peaked between the 1960s and 1980s before a sharp decline during the compact disc era. Over recent decades, vinyl releases have experienced a massive resurgence as dedicated collectors seek physical records once again.

Genre Distribution Across Decades

Musical tastes shifted dramatically across generations. Rock and pop dominated vinyl production throughout the 1970s and 1980s, while genres like Electronic, Folk, and Hip-Hop gained noticeable traction in modern decades.
