The comeback

Vinyl sales have surged since 2011.

ARIA records what Australians buy each year.

These three charts track the revival of vinyl collecting.

Together they show growth, a milestone and a shift.

Chapter 2

What gets pressed

Discogs catalogues records from around the world.

We counted Australian vinyl releases by year and genre.

Counts show releases, not sales.

These charts compare genres and release years.

They reveal how music preferences shifted across decades.

Who makes the music

Chapter 4

Where to buy it

We mapped stores listed by Record Store Day Australia.

The list is not every record shop.

These maps ask where fans can buy vinyl.

Vinyl went from niche to most physical sales.

Classics now sit beside new pop hits.

Taste changes, but the record keeps spinning.

Vinyl album sales grew about 26 times since 2011.

Vinyl overtook CDs in revenue in 2021.

Vinyl is now 70 percent of physical revenue.

Today's best sellers mix 1960s classics with new pop.

Yearly releases now exceed five times the 2000s.

Rock has led genre tags in five of seven decades.
